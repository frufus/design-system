The leading bar is the row's status, not decoration. Neutral when there is nothing to say, coloured when there is — and always paired with a word in the trailing column, so it never carries meaning by colour alone.
Two row heights, one grid. Display rows sit at 40 and interactive rows at 52 so the control inside keeps its full 44 px target. Both share the same four columns, which is what keeps the density from reading as clutter.
