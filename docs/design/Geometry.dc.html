Geometry & motion
4 px grid · no shadows anywhere
Two radii and two border weights are the whole vocabulary. A panel is a sharp rectangle; only the things you can touch get a corner. Depth is never a shadow — it is whether a surface sits inset or flush, which reads identically in both appearances.

There is no pill and no large radius. A rounded rectangle here means this responds to you, which only works while the surfaces around it stay square.

Density stops at the touch target. Display rows go to 40, but any row holding a control is 52 so the control itself keeps its 44. The 36 size carries an invisible 44 px hit area; shrinking the target is not one of the options.

A shadow is invisible on a near-black ground, so this system never learns to depend on one. Inset means a darker fill and a darker top edge; that reads the same in both appearances.
