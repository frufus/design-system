What happens on open and close
1
Focus enters the dialog — on the close button, unless an element declares itself the initial focus. Never on the destructive action.
2
Tab cycles inside and wraps at both ends. The page behind is inert, so nothing outside can take focus by keyboard or by pointer.
3
Escape and the backdrop both close — and both are the same cancel, never a silent confirm.
4
Focus returns to the trigger on close, even when the dialog closed by Escape or by a backdrop click.
Structure
role="dialog" aria-modal="true"
aria-labelledby → the title slot
aria-describedby → the body slot, when present
Three slots, no strings. Title, body and actions are all supplied by the consumer — including the close button's accessible name. The component ships the behaviour and the geometry; the wording belongs to the project, in whatever language it speaks.
In dark it gains an edge instead of a shadow. Same e3 level, expressed the way that level is expressed in that appearance.
