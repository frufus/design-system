border and border-strong are two different jobs. The hairline separates rows and costs nothing; the strong one is the affordance of a control and a panel edge, so it carries the 3:1 non-text floor. Measured at 3.82 in light and 3.23 in dark.
Cyan cannot hold chroma at mid lightness. That is why the light accent sits at L 0.518 rather than the 0.568 every other direction uses — white on the primary fill measured 4.30 before the move, and 5.32 after.
