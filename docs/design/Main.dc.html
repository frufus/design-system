@frufus/design-system
One material, not two. Every neutral carries the accent hue at trace chroma (268°, C 0.004–0.019), so surfaces and accent read as the same substance under different light. Light and dark are equal rank: each is designed and measured, neither is derived from the other.

border-strong is darker than a soft system usually wants. An input's border is its only affordance, so it carries the 3:1 non-text floor rather than a decorative one — measured, not assumed. The airy border stays available as border for card edges and dividers, where the component is identifiable without it.
